Who We Are

How we started

North Star Bakery started in 2009 as a single oven and a weekend market table. Neighbors kept coming back for the bread and that morning line grew into a storefront. The name comes from the star that guided travelers home, and that is still what we hope to be for the people who live around us.

Our founder, Todd, spent years baking bread at home for friends and neighbors. Todd wanted a shop where everyone in the neighborhood felt welcome and where the bread tasted like it had been made by hand.

Our sourcing philosophy

  • Flour is milled by a regional mill that we visit each year.
  • Dairy, eggs and seasonal fruit come from farms within a short drive of the shop.
  • We keep ingredient lists short and label common allergens on every item
  • Unsold bread goes to a local food pantry at the end of every day.

Meet the team

Todd, Owner

Todd opened North Star Bakery in 2009 and still works the counter most Saturday mornings. Stop by and ask about the neighborhood history behind the stroefront.

Agnes, Head Baker

Agnes has been baking here for over a decade and still starts most mornings before sunrise. Ask Agnes for a recommendation and you will usually be pointed to the sourdough

Tyler, Lead Pastry

Tyler leads our pastry kitchen and is known for the chocolate chip sandwich cookies. On weekends Tyler experiments with seasonal fruit tarts that often end up on next week's menu.

Sophia, Front of House

Sophia is usually the first voice customers hear. Sophia remembers regulars' orders and helps first time visitors choose a loaf amd knows which cakes need extra lead time for event orders.
